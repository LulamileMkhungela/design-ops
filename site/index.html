Agent-first linter for Tailwind design systems

Write design system rules that agents can verify.

You define what’s allowed. When an agent breaks a rule, the error explains what’s wrong and suggests a fix — from your components, variants, and theme. Works with your existing design system. No rewrite required.

    Evals

    Measured with agents, not just asserted.

    150+ task runs across models: nearly every task reached zero violations in one correction round — and lint feedback cost 10–48% less than rules-alone in the Claude control runs.
